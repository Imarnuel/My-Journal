# UI audit, 22 November 2026 (ui-polish, first run)

Method: `.agents/skills/ui-polish` — `scripts/ui-lint.mjs` on the code, `scripts/ui-shots.mjs` for 26 screens
and states in light and dark (52 screenshots at 2x), each reviewed against `CHECKLIST.md` with pixel
measurements; the most visible findings were checked again by hand. Grouped by the rule that fixes them on every
screen. Coordinates are CSS px at 1440 wide. Letters refer to the reviewers' crops (A dashboard and trade log,
B stories, C playbook to start, D the real journal).

## Fixes (no new wording)

1. **Popovers and menus sit on their trigger and inside their column**
   - The date and time popover opens 110px left of its trigger, hangs outside the Details card over the Add tile,
     the Notes card and Done; its content has about 5px padding (B-1).
   - The Details fields (Setup, Headspace) are shifted 12px left instead of widened, so their right edge, and
     their menus', stop 12px short of the column (B-2); their chevrons sit 29px inside the edge (D-4).
   - The Pair menu's scroll edge cuts through a group label with a sliver of the next row (B-5).
2. **Text buttons line up by their visible text**, not their padding: "Edit session times" and "Manage tags"
   start 12px in (C-3a, D-5); "Add another" on Start and "Delete" on Edit account end 12–13px short (C-3b, C-3c);
   the Rules "Edit" on a setup page ends 14px short.
3. **Text never overflows or leaves a stray separator**: Recent chart stories statuses run into the next card
   and "Not filled" wraps, misaligning the row (A-1); Weekly review tiles wrap with a "·" at the line's end (C-1);
   only the Win rate note wraps on the stat row (A-9).
4. **The same kind of element is the same size**
   - Chips: the grid's Analysis chip is 18px tall against 22px elsewhere (A-5); tag chips 24px against the
     Setup and Headspace chips at 19px (B-3); the Practice chip is a full pill while every chip uses 7px (C-5).
   - Rows: Settings rows are 69, 45 and 65px apart for the same one-line kind (C-4); Dashboard session rows
     73px against setup rows 67px (A-8); the code has list rows at 44, 48, 56, 60 and 64px.
   - Table headers: "Setup" is 14px where every other column header is 12px (A-3).
   - Card padding: the analysis card uses 16px under the Notes card's 24px, so their content edges differ (B-7).
5. **Dark mode is a full equal**: chart thumbnails have no edge and blend into the card (B-8); the Practice
   switch's off track disappears (C-8); the chart drop area's dashed border is barely visible (D-9, both themes).
6. **Actions on an image sit on the image**: the Playbook card's "…" hangs 6px past the thumbnail's corner onto
   the white card, with grey dots instead of white (C-2).
7. **No browser leftovers**: resize grips on the note fields and the review textarea (D-8, C-9); the date field
   shows the browser's "11/16/2026, 09:45 AM" next to the app's "Mon 16 Nov, 09:45" (B-1).
8. **Icons**: the facts line mixes outline icons (pair, direction) with filled ones (status, account, date)
   (B-4); "Before trading" lost its icon in grid cards (A-5); an empty Direction shows the trend-down (Short)
   icon (D-3).
9. **The numbers grid**: the result box is 28px (not 24), its text starts 7px right of the resting value and the
   R runs past its column (D-1); in an empty story the value slots don't share column edges (D-2).
10. **Small offsets and holes**: Start step cards 1px wider or narrower than each other (C-6, D-7); Test
    connection and Reconnect 1px left of the fields (C-7); the Unsorted placeholder 2px narrower (A-2); the
    second thumbnail with no caption leaves a hole in the caption row (B-6); a grid card without a chart is
    mostly blank with its "…" in a different place (A-4); 45px of empty space under the tag chips (B-3); a
    12px label and 14px quote off the same baseline in Weekly review (C-10).
11. **The code check** (`node scripts/ui-lint.mjs`): spacing 6, 7, 10, 14 and 120px; a 12px text on an 18px line
    height and a 14px on 16px; raw colours in `globals.css` and Playbook; `src/lib/tokens.ts` is an unused copy of
    the tokens (delete).
